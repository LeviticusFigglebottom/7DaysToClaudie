# ADR-0048: The Ashen: a human raider faction with camps, scouts, raids, morale and fear of fire

**Status**: Accepted · 2026-10 (phase 1 landed, session 2; phase 2 raids, gardens and saved bands landed; the rest of phase 2 open)

## Context
DESIGN §1, §4 and §6 promise the Ashen: survivors who fled to the high timber, smear themselves with
lichen-ash (it masks their scent from the Hollowed), burn their dead, raise effigies, and kill
outsiders who might carry the Bloom. §6 asks for camps, patrol routes, scouts that observe before
attacking, morale, fear of fire and escalating responses (watchers → raids → war parties).

What exists today:
* `EnemyDef.faction` parses `"ashen"` and `archetype` has `"tribe"`; nothing reads either.
* The Ashen Watch Camp (tier 2) is a camp the fever killed: its sleepers are Hollowed in Ashen
  dress (population `ashen`). Its props (palisade, effigies, pyre, longhouse, hide beds, the
  cache) and `props_outskirts_ashen.py` are a full camp kit.
* `Enemy` targets only the local player. Its only ranged attack is the Blister's spit.
* Hounds (ADR-0034) set the pattern for a new kind of enemy: an `Enemy` with a `behavior.<key>`
  block, a tuning file in `data/config/`, small branches in `enemy.gd`, a game-rule flag, and its
  own test.
* Fire: the player's held flame (`PlayerEquipment.has_flame_on()`) and lit station pieces
  (`BuildingManager._fires`). Camp props' fires aren't registered anywhere.
* `WorldState` keys can be added with no version bump when older saves load them empty (precedent:
  `world.traders`).

## Decision

### Ashen are Enemies with a tribe brain, not a second AI
An Ashen fighter is an `Enemy` with archetype `tribe`, faction `ashen` and a `behavior.tribe` block
(data/enemies/ashen.json). It shares the Hollowed's senses, damage, hit zones, navigation, structure
breaking, XP and kills. What `tribe` changes:
* **Living.** No infection on hit, no Bloom glow, no dawn rooting, no Hum membership, no sleeper
  rise. They bleed. They don't eat corpses.
* **New states**, appended to `Enemy.State` so saved and existing values don't move:
  * `OBSERVE`: a scout holds a vantage 35–55 m from its quarry, with line of sight, still, and
    counts down `observe` seconds. Seen (the player looks at it within `spotted_range`) or
    approached inside `flee_range`, it breaks off.
  * `FLEE`: run for `home` (its camp, or the edge of the spawn ring) and despawn out of sight.
    Morale breaks, and scouts that finished watching, use it.
* **A thrown spear** (`behavior.tribe.throw`): range, cooldown, damage, bleed, an arc. It reuses
  the Blister's projectile path (`Spores.spit` with a spear mesh and no spore puddle) so it can be
  dodged and blocked by cover. Raiders close to melee with an axe or club; scouts throw.
* **Morale** (0–1, `AshenBrain.morale`, pure functions): starts at 1. It drops when a band mate dies
  (`morale.mate_down`), when the fighter is hurt (`morale.hurt` per HP fraction), and every second
  near fire (below). Inside their own camp's territory it never drops under `morale.home_floor`:
  they defend home to the end. Under `morale.break` the fighter flees; the band breaks when half its
  members have fled or died.
* **Fear of fire** (the outsider's fire, not their own pyres): within `fire.radius` of a player
  holding a flame (`has_flame_on()`) and facing them, or of a lit fire or station piece of the
  player's (`BuildingManager` fires), an Ashen loses `fire.morale_per_s` morale and won't step
  inside `fire.keep_off`. It is a real defence at night, as the torch is against hounds, but a band
  that outnumbers the player comes round the far side.
* **Bodies.** `characters/ashen_raider_a`, `_b` and `characters/ashen_scout_a` are generated by the
  Hollowed character pipeline (the same rig and animation set, so they run the Enemy's clips) in
  hide, fur mantle, ash paint and bone, with living skin and no Bloom. `EnemyVisual`'s stand-in
  applies before `make assets`.

### The AshenDirector (`game/src/ai/ashen/`)
A GameWorld module beside the AI director, the one owner of the faction's world state:
* **Camps.** A living camp is a POI def listed in `data/factions/ashen.json` `camps`, with its
  residents (`{enemy: [lo, hi]}`) and its territory radius. Placed buildings come from
  `PoiManager.all_buildings()` (TD-118's PoiRegistry switch applies). Residents are spawned awake,
  round the camp's fires, when the player is within `camp.wake_range`, and kept until
  `camp.sleep_range`; the dead are counted per camp and never come back, so a camp can be wiped
  out. Entering the territory alerts the camp: residents call (a stimulus) and come.
* **Hostility and escalation.** One world-wide score, `hostility`, rises when the player kills an
  Ashen, enters a camp's territory, is reported by a scout, or wipes a camp, and decays each day.
  Its thresholds give the level:
  0 *unaware* → 1 *watchers* (scouts) → 2 *raids* → 3 *war parties*. Each level also needs a
  gamestage (`levels[].gamestage`), so a new player is watched before they are raided.
* **Scouts.** At level 1+, each day rolls a scout visit (deterministic from the world seed and day)
  that comes to the player 60–90 m out, at the base when there is one. A scout that finishes
  watching and gets away raises hostility and marks the base for the next raid; one killed raises
  it less.
* **Raids.** At level 2+, a raid comes at dusk on a day the roll picks (`raid.chance` by level),
  never on a Hum day or night, never while the Hum is active, and never on day 1–`raid.grace_days`.
  A raid needs a base (pieces placed by a player; the Hum's `_find_base` rule) or comes to the
  player. The band (`raid.size` by level, scaled by gamestage) spawns 90–120 m out on the side
  away from the base's lights and walks in; it breaks structures in its way like the Hollowed,
  targets the player first, and retreats when its morale breaks. At level 3 the band is a war
  party: bigger, with a firebrand whose blows set fire damage on structures.
* **Heat.** The Ashen don't answer heat directly (that stays the Hollowed's), but a heat trigger
  inside a camp's territory raises hostility. Fights with them make noise like any other, so a raid
  repelled with guns draws Hollowed heat responses.
* **The Hum.** Ashen out when the Hum starts flee (they know what it is), and no scouts or raids
  are scheduled for a Hum day.
* **Traders.** No Ashen spawn or walks inside a post's safe zone (`spawn_point_ok` already); the
  guards shoot Ashen inside it too.
* **Settings.** World settings `ashen` (on/off) and `ashen_aggression` (`calm`, `normal`, `fierce`:
  a multiplier on hostility gain and raid chance), in a new `factions` category.

### Save
`world.ashen` (new key, loads empty from older saves, no version bump; v7 stays session 3's):
`{hostility, level, last_raid_day, base_marked, camps: {building id: {dead: n, alerted: bool}}}`.
A raid or a scout in progress isn't saved; it is simply over after a reload.

### Content and validation
* `data/enemies/ashen.json` (`ashen_raider`, `ashen_scout`, later `ashen_firebrand`), loot table
  `ashen_remains` (ash pouch, sinew, arrowheads, hide, now and then scrip they took off the dead).
* `data/factions/ashen.json`, a new content kind `faction` (`FactionDef`, `_fields()`): camps,
  levels, raid and scout tuning, morale, fire. Validated: camp POI defs exist, resident enemies exist
  and are faction `ashen`, levels rise.
* **A living camp POI**: `ashen_highcamp` (tier 3): the kit's palisade, longhouse, smoke hut, pyre
  and effigies, lit fire pits, a cache. It enters the random-world wilderness pool (`remote`,
  `min_danger` 3). Its props' fires register as lights.
* New props from a generator in tools/assetgen (war drum, spear rack, ash-pot), never third-party.
* **XP and directives**: `repel_raid` XP source; a `raid` directive event and directive
  "Turn back the Ashen" (chapter 3).
* **Field manual**: a "The Ashen" page.
* **QA shot**: `ashen_highcamp`.

### Not in phase 1 (phase 2, later)
* Ashen and Hollowed fighting each other (a faction relation matrix and a target abstraction in
  `Enemy`, which today only targets the player), luring Hollowed into a camp, Ashen burning nests.
* Territory influence that shifts over weeks; alliance, trade and Ashen contracts; the main map's
  F2 and F4 regions.
* Bows (the spear throw stands in), patrol routes between camps, a living-human animation set.

### Phase 2: raids that path, hit empty bases, raid gardens and survive a save (TD-188, TD-189, TD-211)
* **The way in.** A raid on a base picks its goal: the weakest piece within `base_range` (fewest
  hit points, ties by id; never a lit fire or a garden bed). AshenDirector builds a `FlowField`
  round the base (the Hum's class, `raids.flow`: structures cost hp × `structure_cost_per_hp` to
  cross, lit fires `fire_cost` out to `fire.keep_off`, beds and the goal nothing) integrated to the
  goal, synchronously (a few thousand cells, deterministic). Enemy needs no new state: every tick
  the director gives each raider at a loose end (IDLE, WANDER, INVESTIGATE) a waypoint a few cells
  down the field (`notice`), or, in reach of the goal, sets it breaking that piece; when the goal
  falls the next weakest is picked and the field rebuilt. A raider that sees the player still
  turns on them first.
* **The dark side.** The band comes out within `raids.dark_side` degrees of the bearing opposite
  the summed directions of the base's lit fires (`dark_bearing`); with none, anywhere on the ring.
* **Nobody home.** The director remembers the base the player was last at (`ashen.base`); a raid
  due while they are away goes there. Once the band has arrived, broken `sack_pieces` pieces (or
  had none to break) and taken every ripe crop, it leaves (not repelled: no XP, `raid_ended` with
  `false`). Raiders of any finished raid are followed until they get away and then despawned.
* **Gardens.** A raider within `gardens.reach` of a bed tramples it once a raid and takes its ripe
  crops (`FarmManager.raid_bed`, which writes WorldState.farms like the farm commands); the last
  `gardens.looters` of the band go for ripe beds first.
* **Saved bands.** On `Events.game_saving` the raid (members' enemy ids, positions, morale and
  jobs; goal, goal piece, elapsed time, trampled beds) and the scouts (position, watched time,
  morale) go into `world.ashen.live` (no version bump); the director respawns them as themselves on
  its first tick after a load. Raid and scout bodies get fixed ids (`ash:raid:<day>:<n>:<i>`,
  `ash:scout:<day>:<n>`) so a restored body never collides with the AI director's running count.
  Ashen already running off are not saved. This replaces phase 1's "a raid or scout in progress
  isn't saved".

## Consequences
* The wilderness gets a second, thinking enemy that answers to what the player does: where they
  build, whom they kill, whether they carry fire.
* Raids give base building a reason between Hums without competing with them.
* `enemy.gd` grows a third behaviour block after hounds; the shared parts stay shared.
* Gaps are TD-186..195.
